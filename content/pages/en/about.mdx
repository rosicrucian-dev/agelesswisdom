# About

This project was created to centrally organize the early curriculum of Paul Foster Case and present the lessons in a more accessible format for the modern era. The source material for these lessons was generously contributed by Tony DeLuce.

This website is a project of [Rosicrucian Developers](https://rosicrucian.dev). For questions, contact the lead developer, [Jonathan](https://rosicrucian.dev/jonathan).

## Downloads

The complete curriculum is available to download as PDF:

- [The School of Ageless Wisdom as individual PDF files](/downloads/all-lessons.zip)
- [The School of Ageless Wisdom in one PDF file](https://github.com/rosicrucian-dev/books/releases/download/latest/school-of-ageless-wisdom.pdf)

The School of Ageless Wisdom was previously published under these titles:

- Occult Fundamentals and Spiritual Unfoldment
- Esoteric Secrets of Meditation and Magic
- Wisdom of Tarot
- Tarot Revelations
- Hermetic Alchemy
